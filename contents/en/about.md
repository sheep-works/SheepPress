## Services

### Translation Direction & Scheduling
In projects requiring simultaneous translation into multiple languages, high-volume file processing, or task division under tight deadlines, distributing work efficiently among multiple translators is crucial to maximizing performance.

At Lambuage, our team members have hands-on experience as both professional translators and Project Managers (PMs). Acting as a bridge between clients and translators, we serve as facilitators to ensure smooth workflows even in complex projects.

---

### Translation + Value-Added Services
Translated text cannot always be used directly as plain text. It often needs to be reflected in Office files like PPT, Word, and Excel, imported into specialized design software like Illustrator or AutoCAD, or converted into video subtitles.

We offer comprehensive post-translation DTP and engineering support.

---

### Translation Tool Development
In collaboration with "Sheep Translation Studio" (ひつじの翻訳室), Lambuage develops handy tools for translators such as "TraToys" and project management systems for small translation agencies like "Sheep Accounting".

We also utilize VBA, Python, and other technologies to automate pre-processing and post-processing tasks for client Office files.

---

### Towards Sustainable Translation
With machine translation and generative AI advancing rapidly, Lambuage views these technological shifts as great opportunities.

We actively organize study groups on leveraging new technologies and mentoring salons for emerging linguists, striving to contribute to long-term global communication and competitiveness.
