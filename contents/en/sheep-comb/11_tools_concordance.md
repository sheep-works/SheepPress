## Utility Tools

Standalone utility tools accessible from the "Tools" menu, independent of the main bilingual workflow.

### Concordance Search

Instantly searches across large volumes of loaded bilingual data to locate every sentence containing a specific word or phrase.

- **Key Use Cases**:
  - Review how a specific term was translated in past projects.
  - Verify consistency and terminology variation across large corpora.
- **Key Features**:
  - Blazing-fast 1-character search across Japanese, Chinese, and European languages.
  - Export search indexes to reload search sessions instantly without re-uploading source files.
