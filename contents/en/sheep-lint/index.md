# SheepLint

An AI-powered text proofreading and quality verification tool.

The concept is to remove **Lint** (fluff/loose threads).

It cleanly scrubs away errors and oversights clinging to translated text.

## Key Features

- **High-Precision QA**: Utilizes advanced AI models to flag typos, omissions, and grammatical/contextual inconsistencies.
- **Customizable**: Allows defining custom project-specific terminology and validation rules.
- **High-Speed Processing**: Scans large volumes of bilingual text in moments.
